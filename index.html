LOCAL FIRST. CLEAR ABOUT THE LIMITS.

Your media belongs
on your terms.

No Vibe Wire cloud account, telemetry, advertising or automatic crash uploads. Local storage and understandable controls, with clear limits.

Your data stays local.

Settings, library records, artwork and playback history are stored on your computer. A folder you choose may separately be synced by Windows or another service.

Online actions have a purpose.

Downloads, metadata previews, explicit dependency updates and enabled online services need connections. Local music tags and visualizers do not.

Visibility and cleanup.

The Privacy Center shows requested domains and observable process connections, with separate local cleanup controls. It is not a complete packet or connection audit.

Credentials deserve care.

Browser-cookie workflows require consent. Saved secrets use Windows user-scoped protection where supported; this does not defend against malware running as your user.

CURRENT LIMITATION

Protected Offline Mode is not available.

OS-enforced network isolation has not passed its validation gates. Do not rely on the offline switch as a firewall guarantee; saved offline intent can also restrict local tools. Websites contacted through yt-dlp can see your IP address and network information.

About this website’s privacy

This static website has no analytics, advertisements, tracking pixels, external fonts, embedded players or contact forms. Its images, scripts and styles load from the same host. Clicking the contact address opens your own email app. If approved for GitHub Pages, GitHub may process visitor connection data under its privacy statement; no claim of anonymous hosting is made.

BUILT ONE THOUGHTFUL STEP AT A TIME

From downloader
to media companion.

An independently developed project, shaped through iteration. These are development milestones, not a catalog of available downloads.

  1. EARLY DEVELOPMENT

    A clearer way to download.

    A desktop home for yt-dlp, readable quality presets, metadata previews and bundled media tools. VLC playback, library management, clipping and conversion expanded the workflow.

  2. 0.3.0 BASELINE

    Privacy becomes a design requirement.

    Local-first storage, a Privacy Center and explicit network controls became core priorities. Later hardening work kept unresolved protected-offline enforcement unavailable.

  3. 0.3.1 BETA

    A workspace that works together.

    The restored baseline gained refined Simple and Advanced modes, tool workspaces, Settings and Privacy tabs, a fixed task status bar and themed scrollbars.

  4. 0.4.0 BETA CURRENT

    The Music Update.

    Five real audio-reactive visualizers, local tags and album artwork, plus opt-in online metadata infrastructure. The application remains in beta; live provider retrieval is still being validated.

Patch numbers are for fixes and small polish. Minor versions mark substantial new features. Experimental builds use numbered beta labels; 1.0.0 is reserved for a genuinely ready stable public release.

INDEPENDENT BY DESIGN

Made for the way
you use your media.

Vibe Wire brings capable open-source media engines into one approachable Windows application. The goal is practical: fewer disconnected steps between finding media, enjoying it and making something with it.

Developed independently by oofitsnathaniel. Built on yt-dlp, FFmpeg and VLC, with Deno supporting applicable download workflows. Vibe Wire is not affiliated with those projects or with media websites.
